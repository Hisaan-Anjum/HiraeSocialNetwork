30 things to do on FaceTime with your long-distance partner

Talking every night is lovely until you run out of things to say. The calls that feel closest usually have a job: something to watch, play, make or plan together. Here are thirty, from lazy weeknights to proper dates.

Watch something together

  • 1. A film night. Our 21 movie night ideas turn it into a ritual, and there's a list of 25 films to start with.
  • 2. Start a series from episode one. One episode a night gives the week a shape.
  • 3. Rewatch your favourite childhood film and narrate why it mattered.
  • 4. Trailer roulette. Watch five trailers, pick this weekend's film.
  • 5. A YouTube rabbit hole. Take turns choosing the next video.

One catch: screen sharing Netflix, Prime or Disney+ on FaceTime or Discord usually shows a black screen because of DRM (why). Each of you playing it yourselves works; see how to watch movies together long distance.

Play something

  • 6. skribbl.io or Gartic Phone. Drawing-and-guessing games in the browser; free and chaotic.
  • 7. GeoGuessr. Guess where in the world you've been dropped. Surprisingly competitive.
  • 8. Online chess or Codenames. Slow enough to talk through.
  • 9. 20 questions or "would you rather". No setup, works on any call.
  • 10. A pub quiz for two. Each writes ten questions about the other.
  • 11. A co-op video game on whatever you both own.

Make something

  • 12. Cook the same recipe at the same time and compare results on camera.
  • 13. Bake-off. Same cake, secret decorating.
  • 14. Paint or draw along with the same tutorial.
  • 15. Make each other a playlist and play it back song by song.
  • 16. Write each other a letter on the call, then post it.

Talk, but differently

  • 17. The 36 questions. From psychologist Arthur Aron's study on closeness; they get deeper as you go.
  • 18. "Best and worst of the week" every Sunday.
  • 19. Tell each other a story from before you met that the other hasn't heard.
  • 20. Read the same book and talk a chapter at a time.

Plan the future

  • 21. Plan your next visit day by day, with a map open.
  • 22. A shared bucket list of places you'll go together.
  • 23. Design your dream home, room by room, and argue about the sofa.
  • 24. Budget for closing the distance. Less romantic, very bonding.

For busy weeks (low effort, still together)

  • 25. Work or study on the call. Say nothing for an hour; it still counts.
  • 26. Fall asleep on the call. A classic for a reason.
  • 27. Take them on your walk or grocery run.
  • 28. Get ready together before a night out.
  • 29. Tidy your rooms together with a playlist on.
  • 30. Stargaze with a free sky app, each from your own window.

Questions people ask

What can we do on FaceTime besides talking?

Give the call a job: a film in sync, a browser game, the same recipe, a plan for your next visit. Doing something side by side often feels closer than talking the whole time.

How do we watch a movie together on FaceTime?

Don't share your screen for Netflix, Prime or Disney+; it goes black. Each play it on your own account and press play together, or use a sync extension.

How do we stop calls getting boring?

Mix low-effort calls where you each do your own thing with one proper themed date a week. The ritual matters more than the activity.
