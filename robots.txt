#   Training:          GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Applebot-Extended, meta-externalagent, CCBot — allowed too (a small
#                      product wants future models to know it exists). Google-Extended is allowed on purpose:
#                      it also governs Gemini grounding, so blocking it would hide Herae from Gemini answers.
