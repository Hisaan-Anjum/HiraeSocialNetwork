What it doesn’t do.

Not on phones

It is a desktop Chrome extension. If you two mostly watch on phones in bed, this genuinely is not for you yet.

It takes two

Both of you install it. That is the honest reason most people put it off, and there is no version of this that works from one side.

The camera, before you ask

Face detection runs inside your own browser. No video or image is ever sent anywhere, no record of your face is kept, and nothing leaves your computer until you choose to keep a moment. One switch turns it off entirely, and off means the model is unloaded — no camera, no CPU, no memory.

Good questions, answered.

What exactly is Herae?

Herae is an app that lets you watch anything together, in perfect sync, on almost any website — with voice, video, and chat built right in. It's made for long-distance couples, friends, and family who want a real night in from anywhere.

How is it different from screen sharing?

Screen sharing streams a laggy, blurry copy of your screen and drains your battery. Herae keeps everyone on their own crisp, full-quality video and just syncs the play, pause, and skip — so it looks perfect for everyone at once, and you can actually talk over it.

Does it really work on any website?

Almost any. Unlike watch-party tools locked to one or two streaming services, Herae syncs video across nearly any site — including embeds most other tools can't reach. Wherever the video already is, Herae works there.

Is it free?

Yes — Herae is free to start. Add it to Chrome, create an account in seconds, and start watching together.

What are Herae Moments?

Herae Moments is your shared, private timeline. After every session you can capture photos, save reaction videos, review what you watched, name the night, and like or comment on it later — so your movie nights add up into a scrapbook of your relationship you can relive any time, for years.

What do I need to get started?

Just Chrome on a computer, and someone to watch with. Add the free extension, sign in, and open the same page together — that's it.

Which streaming services does Herae work with?

Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Apple TV+, Disney+, Hulu, Max, YouTube, Crunchyroll, Vimeo — and almost any other site that plays video in a browser. Herae syncs the video itself rather than integrating with each service one at a time, which is why it also works on small, self-hosted and independent sites that other watch-party tools simply don't support.

Does it work with videos inside iframes?

Yes — including cross-origin embeds and players nested several levels deep. This is where most watch-party extensions give up: they support a fixed list of services and fail the moment the player is embedded somewhere unexpected.

What happens during ad breaks?

You stay together. Ad breaks are the single most common reason watch parties drift apart — one person's ads finish before the other's and you spend the next scene out of step. Herae accounts for pre-roll and mid-roll interruptions and brings you both back to the same frame.

How is Herae different from Teleparty?

Teleparty supports a fixed list of major services and has text chat only. Herae syncs the video element directly — so it works on almost any site, inside iframes, and through ads — and has video and voice built in. And Herae keeps what happened: every night becomes part of a private shared archive. No other watch-party tool does that.

Is Herae watching me through my camera?

Nothing leaves your computer. The face detection runs entirely inside your own browser — no video, no images, and nothing derived from them is ever sent to a server for analysis. It looks for the shape of a reaction, not for who you are, and it keeps no record of your face. The only thing that is ever saved is a moment you can see, delete or turn off completely.

Who can see my moments?

Only the two of you, unless you decide otherwise. Every moment is private by default. If you choose to share one, it gets a link with a preview — and if you later make it private again, that link stops working.
