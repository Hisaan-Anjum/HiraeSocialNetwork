A Teleparty alternative that works on almost any site

We make Herae, so read this knowing that. We've tried to be straight about where Teleparty is the better pick, because it sometimes is.

Why people look for a Teleparty alternative

Two reasons come up again and again:

  • The site isn't supported. Teleparty works with a list of services, and it needs specific support for each one. If the film you want is on a smaller streaming site, or in a player embedded inside another page, it just sits there doing nothing.
  • Video chat costs extra. On Teleparty's free plan you get text chat. Seeing each other while you watch needs Premium.

Stay with Teleparty if…

  • you only watch on the big services it supports, and text chat is enough;
  • you want to watch on phones or tablets;
  • you watch a lot and want the cheapest paid plan per person.

Try Herae if…

  • the site you actually watch on isn't on anyone's supported list;
  • you want to see each other while you watch, without paying for it;
  • your partner won't make yet another account (they can join from a link);
  • you'd like to keep the moment they laughed, not just the film title.

Other alternatives worth knowing

  • Rave: apps on phones and computers, with its own supported services.
  • Watch2Gether: good for YouTube and similar sites, in the browser.
  • Hyperbeam: a shared virtual browser, so one person streams to everyone.
  • The countdown: free, works everywhere, falls apart at the first pause. More in our long-distance guide.

Questions

Does Herae work on Netflix?

Yes. Each of you plays it on your own account, and Herae keeps the two players in step. Netflix titles have to be available in both of your countries.

Why is my screen share black?

Netflix, Prime and Disney+ block screen capture, so Discord and Meet show black. Neither Herae nor Teleparty shares the picture; each of you plays your own copy, so there's nothing to go black.
