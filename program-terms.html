Part B · Creator & Affiliate Program

B1. Joining

The program is by invitation. To join, you need a Herae account and must be at least 18 and able to enter a contract. If you join on behalf of a business or a team, you confirm you're allowed to bind it. You join when you confirm in writing (an email reply is enough) that you accept these terms, and we confirm you're in. Your written confirmation, the version of these terms you accepted, and your agreed rate are kept on record.

B2. What you earn

Unless we agree a different rate or period with you in writing, you earn 30% of the Net Revenue from each Referred Couple's payments made in the first 6 months after that couple's first payment.

  • A Referred Couple is an account created through your Herae invite link, together with the partner account connected to it, where the account didn't already exist and wasn't referred by someone else first. Only the first referral link used counts.
  • Net Revenue is what Herae actually receives for a payment after sales tax and VAT, payment-processing and Merchant-of-Record fees, currency conversion, refunds and chargebacks. Our payment provider, Paddle, is the reseller of Herae plans, and Net Revenue is the payout Paddle makes to Herae for that payment.
  • You earn nothing from free sign-ups, free nights, gift links you were given, free plans, or couples who never pay. Commission only ever comes from money a Referred Couple actually paid.
  • You can't earn commission on your own account, your partner's, or anyone's you control.

B3. When commission becomes payable

A payment counts once it is 30 days old and hasn't been refunded or charged back. That covers the refund window. If a payment is refunded or charged back after you've been paid for it, that amount is taken off your future commission. It is never taken back from you in any other way, unless it came from fraud under A2.

B4. Getting paid

  • We pay payable commission monthly, within 30 days after the end of each month, once your balance is at least US$25. Smaller balances roll over. Payment is in US dollars by PayPal, Wise or bank transfer, whichever we agree with you.
  • We pay our sending fees. Your receiving fees and any currency conversion on your side are yours.
  • You're responsible for your own taxes on what you earn. We may ask for the details we need to pay you or to meet tax or anti-fraud rules, for example your legal name, country and payout details. We can hold a payment until we have them.
  • With each payment we'll show how it was worked out: the number of paying Referred Couples, the Net Revenue, and your share. If you think a number is wrong, tell us within 60 days of that payment and we'll look at it with you.
  • What happens to your balance when you leave, are removed, or the program or Herae ends is set out in B8.

B5. How you promote Herae

  • Disclose it. Wherever you mention Herae in exchange for commission or free access, say clearly that it's a paid or affiliate relationship. Use your platform's paid-partnership label and/or plain words like "ad" or "I earn from this link". This is required by the US FTC's Endorsement Guides, the UK CAP Code and similar rules elsewhere.

Part C · Invite rewards (referral program)

  • Someone who signs up through your link, as a new account, gets an extra free movie night.
  • You get an extra free night the first time someone who joined through your link watches a movie night of 45 minutes or more with their partner (not with you). That person is then a qualifying couple. A referral has to qualify within 90 days of the sign-up.
  • When 3 of your invited couples have qualified, you and your partner get one month of Herae Together free. This is given once per account, and nothing is ever charged for it.
  • Invite rewards are free access (A1). They don't renew into a paid plan and end as A3 and A4 describe.

Part D · Gifts ("a year of movie nights")

D1. Purchase. Gifts are sold through Paddle as Merchant of Record, as for our plans. You get a gift link, and we email it to you. The price is shown before you pay. A gift is a one-time payment and never renews.

D2. What it gives. Whoever opens the link and signs in gets 365 days of Herae Together for their account and their partner's. If their partner connects later, the partner gets the rest of that year. A link works once.

D3. When it starts. An unopened paid gift link has no expiry date. The year starts when the link is opened. Like everything Herae offers, it depends on Herae operating (A4).

D4. Refunds. The buyer can get a full refund within 14 days of purchase if the link hasn't been opened, and the link then stops working. After that, our Refund Policy and your statutory rights apply. If the link is lost or shared by mistake before anyone opens it, email us from the address you bought with and we'll cancel it and issue a new one.

D5. If Herae closes. Gifts end when the service ends (A4). Refunds are given where the law requires them, for example a consumer's statutory right to a refund for a paid service they didn't receive.

D6. Free gift links. Links that Herae gives away for free, for example through a creator's giveaway, work in the same way but aren't refundable and have no cash value. Unless the post that gave them out says otherwise, they're valid for 12 months from when they were given.

  • A grant covers the account it was given to and, where we say so, the partner account connected to it.

  • A grant is a gift from Herae, not payment for anything. You don't have to post about Herae or say anything positive to keep it. If you do mention Herae publicly after receiving free access, disclose it as B5 describes.
  • Like every program, free plans can be changed or ended by Herae (A3), and they end if Herae stops operating (A4). Nothing is owed when they end. We'd only end a free-for-life plan early for a serious breach of the Terms & Conditions, abuse under A2, or if the plan itself is discontinued for everyone.
