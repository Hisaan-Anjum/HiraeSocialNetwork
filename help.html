How Herae works

Watch something together from anywhere, see each other while you do, and keep the moments worth remembering.

  1. 1 Install the extension It lives in your browser toolbar and works on the sites you already watch on.
  2. 2 Add each other Send an invite link. Whoever opens it appears in your contacts — no account needed to start.
  3. 3 Watch together Play, pause and seek happen for both of you. Talk over video or voice while it plays.
  4. 4 Keep the good bits Herae notices real reactions and offers them at the end of the night. What you keep lands here.
