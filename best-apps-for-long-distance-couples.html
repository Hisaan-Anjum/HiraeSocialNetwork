Best apps for long-distance couples in 2026, by what you actually need

No single app does everything, and most couples who make distance work use two or three. The useful question isn't "which app is best" but "which gap are we filling": staying close day to day, having a shared space, feeling each other's presence, or actually spending an evening together.

1. Daily connection (five minutes a day)

  • Agapé. One research-backed question a day for both of you to answer. Low effort, good for busy weeks.
  • Paired. Daily questions and quizzes (love languages, attachment and conflict styles) with expert-reviewed content.
  • Connected. Daily conversation prompts, mood tracking, quizzes, a shared journal and date-night planning; free tier plus a premium plan that covers both partners.
  • Amora. A daily question you answer privately before seeing each other's answer, a shared journal, widgets, date reminders, and a "Passport" of countries you've visited together.
  • Lovewick. Decks of discovery questions and distance-friendly date ideas.

2. A shared space for photos, plans and memories

  • Between. A private space for the two of you: chat, photos and a relationship timeline.
  • Couple (Couple Joy). A private feed of photos, voice notes and doodles, moments pinned on a map, and a home-screen widget.
  • Cupla. A shared calendar built for couples, with your work schedules side by side so plans (and visits) actually happen.

3. Feeling each other's presence

  • Locket. The other person's latest photo on your home screen, all day.
  • Touch bracelets and lamps (such as Bond Touch). Tap yours and theirs lights up or vibrates, anywhere in the world.
  • Lovebox. A little box at home that shows the notes and photos they send.

4. Spending an actual evening together

This is the gap the other categories don't fill: sitting through a film together. Screen sharing Netflix, Prime or Disney+ on FaceTime or Discord usually shows a black screen because of DRM, so these tools keep each person's own player in sync instead.

  • Teleparty. The best-known sync extension: Netflix, Disney+, Max, Prime and others, with a text chat; video chat and more services on its premium plan.
  • Herae (ours, full disclosure). Press play once and the film plays for both of you, with a video call right next to it, on almost any site including smaller ones. It keeps the moments you both reacted to on a timeline of your own. Chrome on a computer; the first two movie nights are free.

Questions people ask

What's the best app for a long-distance relationship?

A combination: a daily-connection app, a shared space, and something for evenings together. No single app covers all three.

How do long-distance couples watch movies together?

Each plays the film on their own account and a sync tool keeps it matched. Screen sharing Netflix usually goes black.

Is there an app to feel your partner's presence?

Touch bracelets and lamps, photo widgets like Locket, and message boxes like Lovebox all do this in different ways.
