Why Netflix is a black screen on Discord (and what actually works)

You share your screen, press play, and the other person sees black with the sound still playing. It isn't your settings, and it isn't Discord.

Why it happens

Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, Max and most paid services protect their video with DRM. The browser plays the film through a protected path, and when a screen-capture tool asks for the picture, it gets a black frame. Discord, Google Meet, Zoom, WhatsApp and FaceTime all see the same thing. Free sites and YouTube usually share fine, because they don't use that protection.

What about turning off hardware acceleration?

Most guides you'll find tell you to switch off hardware acceleration in your browser and in Discord. It sometimes lets the picture through. Before you rely on it:

  • It works by getting around the service's content protection, which Netflix's terms of use (section 1.8) ask members not to do.
  • It can stop working with any browser or Discord update.
  • Your processor now decodes the video, which can make playback choppier on the sending side.
  • The other person still gets a compressed copy of your screen: 720p on free Discord, and only as good as your upload speed.

What works without any workaround

Don't send the picture at all. Each of you plays the film on your own account, and only the timing is shared. That's also what Netflix's terms expect: the service isn't meant to be shared outside your household, so each of you on your own account is the straightforward way.

The countdown. Get on a call, load the film, press play on three. Free and it works everywhere. It drifts over a long film, and every pause means another countdown.
A sync extension. It keeps play, pause and skip in step for you. Nothing is captured, so nothing goes black, and each of you watches at full quality. Teleparty and Herae both work this way (how they compare).

One catch for any of these: the title has to be available to both of you. If you're in different countries, check that it shows up in both catalogues.
