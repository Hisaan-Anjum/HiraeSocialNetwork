How to watch Netflix together long distance (without the black screen)

Netflix doesn't have a "watch together" button of its own. If you're in different cities or countries, these are the three ways people actually do it, and what each one costs you.

1. A countdown on a call

Both open the same title, get on a call, and press play on three. It's free and works on TVs and phones. The catch: you drift apart over a long film, and every pause or bathroom break needs a new countdown.

2. Screen sharing (and why Netflix goes black)

Netflix uses DRM, so when Discord, Google Meet, Zoom or FaceTime asks the browser for the picture, it gets a black frame. That's deliberate, and no setting fixes it. Even when the sound comes through, the person watching your share sees nothing. More in why Netflix goes black on Discord.

3. A sync extension: each of you plays your own Netflix

Each of you plays the film on your own Netflix account, at full quality, and a browser extension keeps play, pause and skip together. That's how Teleparty (which started life as Netflix Party) and Herae work. With any of them, each of you needs Netflix, and the title has to be in both your countries' catalogues.

Herae on Netflix: one of you presses play and it starts on both laptops at the same moment, with a video call right beside the film and a link your partner joins from. Pause, rewind and "wait, what did he say?" stay in step. Honest note: Herae is young, and if playback ever slips out of step, tell us at support@herae.app; we read every message.

Which one should you use tonight?

  • On a TV or phones: the countdown. Extensions need Chrome on a computer.
  • On laptops, and you want to see each other: a sync extension with a call built in, like Herae.
  • A big group of friends: Teleparty's text chat suits a crowd; Herae is built for two and works for a few.

Questions people ask

Does Netflix have a watch party feature?

Not one of its own, as of October 2026. Couples use a countdown or a sync extension.

Why is Netflix black on Discord or FaceTime?

DRM: the browser won't give the picture to screen-capture tools.

Do we both need Netflix?

Yes, each of you plays it on your own account. Sharing one login between two places usually runs into Netflix's household rules anyway.

Can we watch together in different countries?

Yes, as long as the title is available in both. Sync tools can't change a country's catalogue.
