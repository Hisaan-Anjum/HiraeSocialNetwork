How to watch Prime Video together long distance (after Watch Party)

Amazon shut down Prime Video Watch Party on 2 April 2024. If the button is missing, that's why. Here's what works now for two people in different places.

1. A countdown on a call

Both open the same title, get on a call, press play on three. Free, works on TVs and phones, drifts over a long film, and needs a new countdown after every pause.

2. Screen sharing (and why Prime goes black)

Prime Video uses DRM, so when Discord, Google Meet or Zoom asks for the picture, the browser hands over a black frame. That's by design, and no setting fixes it for Prime.

3. A sync extension: each of you plays your own Prime

Each of you plays Prime Video on your own account, at full quality, and a browser extension keeps play, pause and skip together. Teleparty, SyncUp and Herae work this way. For all of them, each of you needs Prime and the title must be available in both your countries.

Herae on Prime Video: it works, with a video call built in, a link your partner can join from, and your nights kept as moments on your timeline. Honest note: Prime's player is fussier than most and Herae is young, so the odd hiccup happens. If playback ever slips out of step, tell us at support@herae.app; we read every message.

What Watch Party did that you might miss

  • Up to 100 people. Watch Party was built for crowds. Herae is built for two, and works for a few friends.
  • The chat. Watch Party had a text chat; Herae has a live video and voice call instead, plus text.
  • Phones and TVs. Extensions need Chrome on a computer. On a TV, use the countdown.
