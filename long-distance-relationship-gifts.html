Long-distance relationship gifts that make the distance feel smaller

The best gifts for a partner far away aren't the most expensive ones. They're the ones that make them feel you're there: something that carries you with it, something you use together, or something that turns your calls into dates.

Gifts that carry your presence

  • Touch lamps. A pair of lamps: tap yours and theirs lights up, anywhere in the world. A quiet "thinking of you" without a message.
  • Open-when letters. A bundle of letters for "open when you miss me", "open when you can't sleep", "open when you've had a bad day". Cheap, handmade, kept for years.
  • Something that smells like you. Your perfume or cologne on a scarf or a hoodie. Oddly powerful.
  • A recorded voice message or a short audiobook of you reading their favourite story.
  • A map print of where you met, or of both your cities with the distance between them.

Gifts that turn calls into dates

  • A movie-night kit. Two identical boxes: the same snacks, the same drink, a cosy blanket, and a note naming the film. You open them on the same call.
  • A cooking kit for two. The same recipe and spices in both kitchens; you cook together on camera.
  • A game you can play apart. A two-player puzzle book, a co-op video game, or a card game with a digital version.
  • A year of movie nights. Full disclosure, this one's ours: a year of Herae Together ($59 once, nothing renews). Press play once and the film plays for both of you, with a video call right next to it, on Netflix, Prime, YouTube or almost any site. Needs Chrome on a computer.

Digital gifts that arrive instantly

  • A playlist with a line about why each song made the cut.
  • A digital scrapbook of your photos and screenshots from your calls.
  • A subscription you'll use together: the same streaming service, a book you both read, a language course in each other's language.
  • A planned visit. Even a date circled on a shared calendar is a gift: something to count down to.

How to choose

  • Think about the gap, not the object. Is it missing each other at night (a lamp, a voice note), feeling apart on weekends (a movie-night kit), or not knowing when you'll next meet (a planned visit)?
  • Mind the post. International shipping is slow and customs can be expensive. Digital gifts and things ordered locally in their country arrive on time.
  • Make it something you share. The gifts couples remember are the ones that became a ritual.

Questions people ask

What is a good gift for a long-distance relationship?

One that creates shared moments: a touch lamp, open-when letters, a movie-night kit, or a year of synced movie nights.

What should I get my long-distance partner for Christmas?

Something that makes the time apart shorter: a shared experience, something that carries you, or something that turns your calls into dates. Order early if it has to cross borders.

What can I give that isn't physical?

A playlist, a voice recording, a digital scrapbook, or a subscription you'll use together. They arrive instantly and skip customs.
